# Ossuary — Music Prompts, Natural Version (one block per track)

The direction approved for the soundtrack: **natural, acoustic, intimate, with a pipe organ and real instruments, and nothing electronic or lo-fi.** A small chamber ensemble in a sealed stone hall: a pipe organ that breathes under everything, a solo cello that sounds like a person, a solo piano, a harp, a celesta or handbell, a clarinet or oboe, soft timpani, and a great deal of silence and space. Beautiful, vast, quiet sorrow, dark and grieving but with a fragile wonder in it, like a ruined cathedral.

The title is built from the structure of the original theme that was loved (the boot, the descent, the hall of bones, the cello and the lie, the seal, the shutdown), translated entirely into natural instruments.

`music-prompts-phosphor.md` (machine hybrid) was rejected as too lo-fi. **This is the version to use.**

## How to use

Each track is **one single prompt**. Copy the whole block inside the grey box and paste it into the generator's style/prompt box with **Instrumental ON**. Nothing else is needed. If a tool limits the length, cut sentences from the **bottom** of the block first.

The prompts deliberately do not name any game, composer or artist, because most generators refuse or distort such names.

## The direction (applies to every block)

- **Natural and acoustic only.** Real instruments in a real room: the felt and hammers of a piano, the bow on a cello, the breath in the organ pipes and the flute. nothing electronic, nothing lo-fi, no bit-crush, no tape hiss, no drum machine.
- **The pipe organ is the backbone.** A small, old organ with the reeds half closed, used for deep drones, slow chords and long sustained notes. It is felt more than heard.
- **Beautiful sorrow.** Melancholic, lonely, tender, hushed, grieving. Never cheerful, never triumphant, never bright.
- **A small chamber ensemble:** pipe organ, solo piano, solo cello, solo violin, harp, celesta, handbell, clarinet or oboe or flute, one low horn, soft timpani, at most a small string quartet. Never a full orchestra, never swelling film-score strings, never brass fanfares, never epic percussion. Never more than four instruments at once (five for the final boss).
- **No vocals of any kind:** no voice, choir, chant, humming, whispering or "ah". Where a choir would sit, use a held organ tone or a long cello note.
- **Space and silence are instruments.** Long pauses, notes left to decay, audible breath and bow noise, a natural stone-hall reverb.
- **One melody, always:** a five-note motif, **D – F – E-flat – D – A**, shifted to other keys where noted. It is always recognisable and never played the same way twice. **The wrong note:** in a few tracks, exactly one E natural for exactly one beat, a bright painful memory, before the E-flat returns.

# Listening test for every generated track

Before accepting a take, check each of these. If any answer is wrong, reject it and regenerate.

1. **Vocals:** is there any voice, choir, humming, chant, whisper or "ah"? Reject.
2. **Natural:** does anything sound electronic, artificial, lo-fi, bit-crushed or like it has tape hiss? Reject.
3. **The organ:** can you feel a low pipe organ breathing underneath, without it being loud? If it is missing in the exploration and dungeon tracks, regenerate.
4. **Mood:** does any part sound cheerful, hopeful, triumphant or adventurous? Reject.
5. **Size:** does it sound like a big orchestra or a movie trailer? It should sound like a handful of musicians in a room. Reject if not.
6. **Space:** is there real silence between phrases, and can you hear the felt of the piano, the breath of the organ and the bow on the cello? If it is wall-to-wall sound, regenerate.
7. **Same game:** is the five-note motif recognisable, and is the feeling beautiful, lonely and grieving?
